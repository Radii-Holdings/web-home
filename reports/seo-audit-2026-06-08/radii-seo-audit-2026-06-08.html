Executive Summary

The site is not primarily suffering from basic crawlability failure. Robots, canonicals, indexing directives, sitemap coverage, HTTPS, and baseline technical SEO are mostly present. In fact, the sampled pages scored 100/100 in Lighthouse SEO.

The bigger problems are CTR, query-intent fit, trust, brand clarity, and live deployment consistency. Google appears willing to index and surface the site, but users are not clicking enough because the commercial pages are not distinct or trustworthy enough in search results, especially for finance-related queries.

Bottom Line

  • Clicks are low because the snippets and page positioning do not sell the click, even when the site appears for relevant terms.
  • Impressions are still limited because the domain has a small number of indexable commercial entry pages and a weak topical hub strategy.
  • Trust signals are not strong enough for a YMYL finance/trading site, and some live content looks unfinished or over-claiming.
  • The live website is also out of sync with stronger SEO work already present in the repo, so some intended improvements are not live.

Why Clicks Are Low

A sitewide average position of 4.6 with a CTR of only 0.2% is a classic sign that the issue is not just ranking. It usually means one or more of these are happening:

  • Google is showing the page, but the title/snippet is not persuasive enough to win the click.
  • The query intent is only partially matched, so the result gets impressions but not trust.
  • The brand is unfamiliar compared with stronger competitors in the same SERP.
  • Google may be rewriting titles because the live H1 and title signals are not aligned cleanly.

Primary click-through blockers

  • Service-page search messaging is generic. Titles are serviceable, but the live H1s are more promotional than query-matching. Example: /algo-trading-platform-india has the title “Algo Trading Platform for Indian Brokers” but the live H1 is “India's Premier Algorithmic Trading Platform.”
  • Most static pages share the same OG/Twitter identity. Homepage, money pages, about, contact, and category hub all reuse the same social title and OG URL pointing at the homepage, while the blog sample has fully page-specific metadata. That means the informational side is technically sharper than the commercial side.
  • Trust pages do not strengthen the snippet. The live /about and /contact pages have no live H1, and the contact page exposes prose-style placeholder form text that looks unfinished when search engines surface it.
  • Brand naming is fragmented. The ecosystem refers to Radii Labs, Layr0, Console, console.radii.in, layr0.org, and aitrade.ing. This weakens brand clarity in a SERP where users want to know exactly who they are clicking.
  • Finance claims may reduce trust. The about page includes “Patent Pending algorithms” and “above 80 percent success rate” language without supporting proof nearby. For a finance site, that can create skepticism rather than clicks.

Why Impressions Are Still Low

The domain is being indexed, but its impression ceiling is still low because the site is not yet set up like a broad, high-authority commercial/search entity.

  • The sitemap only exposes 29 URLs. That is a small indexable footprint for a company trying to grow across multiple trading, quant, and broker-integration themes.
  • The main research hub is noindex. The live navigation points users and crawlers to /categories/all as “Research,” but that page is intentionally excluded from indexing. This removes a natural hub page that could rank for broader topical queries.
  • Commercial pages are not yet fully schema-rich. Blogs have specific article metadata and BlogPosting schema; service pages do not yet present the same level of page-specific structure such as Service, SoftwareApplication, or proof-led entity signals.
  • The site mixes broad macro/investment blog topics with product/service pages. That can generate some impressions, but it dilutes focus unless the blog clusters are tightly tied back to commercial themes and expertise.
  • Freshness signaling is weak. All sampled sitemap URLs share the same lastmod timestamp: 2026-04-11T12:39:54.540Z. That makes freshness updates look mechanical rather than page-specific.
  • Entity authority is still developing. In trading/finance SERPs, impressions often expand only after Google sees strong expertise, clear business identity, proof, citations, backlinks, and consistent brand/product naming.

Technical Reality: This Is Not a “Google Cannot Crawl Me” Problem

The sampled pages are indexable, canonicalized, HTTPS-enabled, and structured well enough to score 100/100 in Lighthouse SEO. That matters because it narrows the diagnosis: the site needs better click-winning SERP assets and stronger authority/trust signals, not just more titles and canonicals.

What To Fix First

Priority Action Expected SEO Effect
1 Deploy the repo's stronger service metadata and verify the rendered live HTML, not just source code. Improves title specificity, SERP clarity, and commercial relevance quickly.
2 Rewrite live service-page H1 and hero copy to match query intent directly and reduce hype language. Improves CTR and lowers title-rewrite risk.
3 Clean up about/contact trust signals: real business email, remove/justify aggressive claims, add proper H1s, fix typos, replace placeholder copy. Improves YMYL trust, user confidence, and click willingness.
4 Decide whether the research hub should become indexable and optimized instead of living as a nav-linked noindex page. Raises broader-topic impression potential.
5 Add page-specific Service / SoftwareApplication schema on money pages. Improves commercial entity clarity and structured understanding.
6 Reduce mobile render delay and above-the-fold weight on homepage and money pages. Supports rankings, engagement, and landing-page quality.
7 Tighten blog clusters around service intents instead of broad low-conversion macro topics alone. Improves topical authority and impression quality.

Expected Outcome If The Right Changes Ship

  • The first improvement should appear in CTR, not just rankings, especially on the five money pages already called out in the repo.
  • Impression growth should follow after Google sees clearer commercial structure, better trust pages, and more consistent internal topical architecture.
  • If nothing changes but the site keeps publishing more broad blogs, impressions may grow slowly, but qualified clicks will likely stay weak.
